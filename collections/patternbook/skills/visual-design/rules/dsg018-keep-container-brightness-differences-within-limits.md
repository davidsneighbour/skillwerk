---
id: DSG018
title: Keep container brightness differences within limits
applies-when: Choosing the colour of a container relative to its background.
---

## Rule

Containers should remain relatively close in brightness to their surrounding backgrounds.

## Check

* Dark interfaces: keep the HSB/HSV brightness difference between container and background within about 12 percentage points.
* Light interfaces: keep the difference within about 7 percentage points.
* Treat these values as visual defaults rather than accessibility contrast requirements.
