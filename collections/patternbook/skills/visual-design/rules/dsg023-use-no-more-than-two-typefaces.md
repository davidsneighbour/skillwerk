---
id: DSG023
title: Use no more than two typefaces
applies-when: Choosing or adding typefaces.
---

## Rule

Limit an interface to a maximum of two typeface families by default.

## Check

* A second typeface should have a clear design purpose.
* Flag additional typeface families unless they are required for specialised content such as code, symbols, or language coverage.
