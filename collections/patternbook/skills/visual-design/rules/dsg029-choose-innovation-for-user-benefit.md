---
id: DSG029
title: Choose innovation for user benefit
applies-when: Introducing a new visual pattern, technology, or interaction instead of an established one.
---

## Rule

Choose a new approach for a concrete user benefit, not novelty alone.

## Check

* Identify the task or experience it improves.
* Compare it with the established solution, including learning effort, accessibility, and maintenance.
* Do not add a new library or experimental interaction solely to make the interface look different.

## Exceptions

Exploratory and expressive work can make novelty part of the brief; keep the intended benefit explicit.
