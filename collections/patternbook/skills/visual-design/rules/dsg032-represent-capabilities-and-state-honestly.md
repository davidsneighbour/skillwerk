---
id: DSG032
title: Represent capabilities and state honestly
applies-when: Presenting product capabilities, availability, progress, results, choices, or promotional emphasis.
---

## Rule

Make presentation match the actual product and its current state.

## Check

* Check that enabled controls, badges, progress indicators, and success messages reflect real behaviour.
* Label examples, estimates, placeholders, and unavailable features so they cannot be mistaken for actual results.
* Flag misleading emphasis, hidden conditions, or unequal choice presentation that steers users through a false impression.

## Sources

* [Dieter Rams: ten design principles, Rams Foundation](https://rams-foundation.org/foundation/design-comprehension/theses/). The interface checks above are Patternbook adaptations, not direct quotations.
