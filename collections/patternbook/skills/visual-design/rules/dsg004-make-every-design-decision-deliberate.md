---
id: DSG004
title: Make every design decision deliberate
applies-when: Setting any visual value, such as spacing, size, colour, border, radius, or shadow.
---

## Rule

Whitespace, alignment, scale, spacing, colour, borders, shadows, and other visual properties should follow a reason or system rather than arbitrary values.

## Check

* Prefer tokens, scales, and documented exceptions over one-off values.
* Flag unexplained visual values that do not relate to the surrounding design system.
