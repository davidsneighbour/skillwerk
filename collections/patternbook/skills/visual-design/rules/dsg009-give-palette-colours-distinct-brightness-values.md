---
id: DSG009
title: Give palette colours distinct brightness values
applies-when: Defining or reviewing palette or accent colours.
---

## Rule

Colours should differ not only by hue but also sufficiently by brightness to avoid competing with each other.

## Check

* Review palette colours in a brightness-based colour model as well as by hue.
* Avoid sets of accent colours with nearly identical brightness unless that is intentional.
