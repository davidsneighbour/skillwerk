---
id: DSG001
title: Use near-black and near-white instead of pure black and white
applies-when: Choosing text, background, or surface colours, especially black or white values.
---

## Rule

Prefer near-black and near-white values over absolute `#000000` and `#ffffff` for normal interface surfaces and text.

## Check

* Treat references to black and white in this rule book as near-black and near-white unless a deliberate exception is specified.
* Flag pure black or pure white when used as a routine UI colour without an explicit reason.
