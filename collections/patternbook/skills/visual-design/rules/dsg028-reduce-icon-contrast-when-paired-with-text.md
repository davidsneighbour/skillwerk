---
id: DSG028
title: Reduce icon contrast when paired with text
applies-when: Placing an icon next to a text label.
---

## Rule

Icons often appear visually heavier than adjacent text and should usually be given less contrast.

## Check

* Reduce icon opacity or adjust its colour when it visually overpowers its accompanying label.
* Preserve sufficient visibility and accessibility while balancing visual weight.
