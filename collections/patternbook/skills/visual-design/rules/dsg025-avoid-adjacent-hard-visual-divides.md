---
id: DSG025
title: Avoid adjacent hard visual divides
applies-when: Separating areas with borders, dividers, or background changes.
---

## Rule

Do not stack multiple strong boundaries next to each other.

## Check

* Avoid combinations such as a container edge immediately beside a divider and a background transition.
* Prefer one clear boundary mechanism for a given separation.
