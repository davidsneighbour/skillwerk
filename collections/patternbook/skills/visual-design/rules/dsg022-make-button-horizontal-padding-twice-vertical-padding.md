---
id: DSG022
title: Make button horizontal padding twice vertical padding
applies-when: Setting button padding.
---

## Rule

Buttons should normally be noticeably wider than they are tall.

## Check

* Default to horizontal padding of about `2 * vertical padding`.
* Treat this as a proportional rule, not a requirement for specific pixel values.
