---
id: DSG010
title: Keep saturated neutrals consistently warm or cool
applies-when: Defining tinted neutral colours.
---

## Rule

If neutrals are tinted, choose one temperature direction for the interface: warm or cool.

## Check

* Do not mix warm-tinted and cool-tinted neutrals within the same palette without a deliberate semantic reason.
