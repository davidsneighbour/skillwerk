---
id: DSG002
title: Saturate neutrals
applies-when: Defining grey or neutral colours in a palette that has a colour direction.
---

## Rule

When the interface has a colour direction, tint neutral greys slightly towards that colour family so the palette feels coherent.

## Check

* Neutral saturation should normally remain subtle.
* In HSB/HSV terms, keep neutral saturation below about 5% unless the design system explicitly specifies otherwise.
