---
id: DSG015
title: Make visually closer surfaces lighter
applies-when: Choosing surface colours for elevated elements, such as cards, menus, or dialogs.
---

## Rule

Use relative lightness to communicate elevation: surfaces that appear closer to the viewer should generally be lighter.

## Check

* Apply the relationship consistently in both light and dark interfaces.
* Elevated surfaces should not become darker than lower surfaces unless the design intentionally uses a different depth model.
