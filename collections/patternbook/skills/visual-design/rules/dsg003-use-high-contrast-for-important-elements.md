---
id: DSG003
title: Use high contrast for important elements
applies-when: Deciding the contrast of content, controls, borders, dividers, shadows, or decoration.
---

## Rule

Use stronger contrast for content and controls that users need to notice. Structural and decorative elements should use less contrast.

## Check

* Primary content, calls to action, and critical controls should visually outrank borders, dividers, shadows, and other supporting elements.
* Do not give low-priority decoration equal or greater contrast than important content.
