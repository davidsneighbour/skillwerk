---
id: DSG020
title: Keep body text at 16px or above
applies-when: Setting the font size of body text.
---

## Rule

Body copy should normally render at an equivalent of at least 16 CSS pixels.

## Check

* Flag body text below 16px unless it serves a secondary or specialised role.
* Relative units such as `rem` are acceptable; evaluate the computed size.
