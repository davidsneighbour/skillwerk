---
id: DSG014
title: Measure spacing between points of high contrast
applies-when: Judging or adjusting the visible space between elements.
---

## Rule

Visual spacing should be judged from the edges users actually perceive, which are usually defined by contrast.

## Check

* Measure apparent spacing between visible high-contrast boundaries, not merely between DOM boxes or background wrappers.
* Re-evaluate spacing when background or foreground contrast changes.
