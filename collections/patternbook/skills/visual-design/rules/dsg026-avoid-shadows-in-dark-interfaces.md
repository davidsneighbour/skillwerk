---
id: DSG026
title: Avoid shadows in dark interfaces
applies-when: Adding depth to elements in a dark theme.
---

## Rule

Do not use drop shadows as the default depth cue in dark themes.

## Check

* Prefer surface lightness, borders, spacing, or another established depth system.
* Allow shadows only when the design explicitly demonstrates that they add useful depth without excessive contrast.
