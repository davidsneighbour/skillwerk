---
id: DSG034
title: Consider resource costs of visual choices
applies-when: Choosing media, fonts, effects, animation, or other resource-intensive visual treatments.
---

## Rule

Consider resource use alongside the visual result.

## Check

* Check whether optional media, font variants, or effects justify their transfer, rendering, and maintenance cost.
* Prefer a lighter treatment when it provides the same useful result, and verify actual differences where practical.
* Do not claim environmental savings from asset size or appearance alone; distinguish measured resource changes from environmental estimates.

## Exceptions

Necessary content, accessibility, and the agreed visual purpose may justify extra resources. Optimise delivery without silently removing them.
