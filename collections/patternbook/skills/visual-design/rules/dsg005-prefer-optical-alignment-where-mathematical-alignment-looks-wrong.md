---
id: DSG005
title: Prefer optical alignment where mathematical alignment looks wrong
applies-when: Centring or aligning icons, shapes, or text inside other elements.
---

## Rule

Mathematical centring is not always visually centred. Shapes, icons, and letterforms may require optical adjustment.

## Check

* Do not force exact geometric alignment when the result visibly looks misaligned.
* Allow small documented offsets for optical alignment.
