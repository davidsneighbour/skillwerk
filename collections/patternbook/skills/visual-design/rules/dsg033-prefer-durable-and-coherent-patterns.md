---
id: DSG033
title: Prefer durable and coherent patterns
applies-when: Replacing established patterns or choosing a visual direction intended for ongoing use.
---

## Rule

Prefer a coherent design that can be maintained beyond a passing trend.

## Check

* Reuse established components and tokens when they meet the task.
* Base a proposed restyle on a concrete usability, content, brand, or maintenance need.
* Consider how the choice holds up across content changes, states, and future additions.

## Exceptions

A time-limited campaign or deliberate visual experiment can justify a short-lived direction. State its scope rather than imposing it on the wider system.
