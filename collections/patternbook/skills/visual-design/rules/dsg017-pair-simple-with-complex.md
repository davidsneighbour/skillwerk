---
id: DSG017
title: Pair simple with complex
applies-when: Placing content on a background image, pattern, or other complex surface.
---

## Rule

Balance visual complexity between foreground and background.

## Check

* Complex backgrounds should carry simple foreground content.
* Complex foreground elements should sit on comparatively simple backgrounds.
* Avoid complex-on-complex compositions unless explicitly designed and tested.
