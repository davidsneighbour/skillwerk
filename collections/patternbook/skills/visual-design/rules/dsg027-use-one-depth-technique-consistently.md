---
id: DSG027
title: Use one depth technique consistently
applies-when: Choosing how elements show elevation or depth.
---

## Rule

Choose a depth language and apply it consistently across the interface.

## Check

* Do not casually mix soft shadows, hard shadows, border-only elevation, and shadowless elevation.
* Components at comparable elevation should use comparable depth treatment.
