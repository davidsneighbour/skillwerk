---
id: DSG019
title: Make outer padding at least as large as inner padding
applies-when: Setting padding in a container that holds several items.
---

## Rule

Within a container, the distance from content to the container edge should be at least as large as spacing between related items inside the container.

## Check

* `outer padding >= inner spacing` should be the default relationship.
* Tighter spacing should communicate stronger relationship between internal elements.
