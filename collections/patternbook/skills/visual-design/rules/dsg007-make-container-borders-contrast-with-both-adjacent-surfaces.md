---
id: DSG007
title: Make container borders contrast with both adjacent surfaces
applies-when: Adding a border to a container that sits on another surface.
---

## Rule

A container border should be visually distinct from both the container surface and the surface behind it.

## Check

* On dark interfaces, the border should normally be lighter than both adjacent dark surfaces.
* On light interfaces, the border should normally be darker than both adjacent light surfaces.
* Avoid border brightness values that sit ambiguously between the two surfaces.
