---
id: DSG021
title: Keep text lines around 70 characters
applies-when: Setting the width of a column that holds prose.
---

## Rule

Optimise readable prose for a line length near 70 characters.

## Check

* Roughly 60-80 characters per line is an acceptable normal range.
* Avoid substantially shorter or longer body-copy measures without a deliberate reason.
* Prefer `ch`-based max widths when appropriate.
