---
id: DSG030
title: Make structure and actions understandable
applies-when: Designing navigation, grouping, controls, labels, or feedback about interface state.
---

## Rule

Make the interface explain what users can do and what happened.

## Check

* Use clear grouping, labels, and state feedback to support the expected task sequence.
* Check that controls communicate their purpose before activation and their result afterwards.
* Do not rely on visual styling alone for a control name or state; preserve semantic and accessible information.

## Sources

* [Dieter Rams: ten design principles, Rams Foundation](https://rams-foundation.org/foundation/design-comprehension/theses/). The interface checks above are Patternbook adaptations, not direct quotations.
