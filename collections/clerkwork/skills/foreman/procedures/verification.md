# Verify a claim

State a falsifiable claim and choose the smallest surface that can disprove it. Use a condition and expected observation; use a metric and threshold when the claim is quantitative.

For a change or improvement claim, compare a valid baseline with the changed state under equivalent inputs and conditions. For an absolute claim, a direct reproducible observation may be sufficient. Do not switch the user's working tree to obtain a baseline; use an isolated checkout or existing artifacts when appropriate.

Collect relevant raw evidence such as test results, request responses, screenshots, or measurements. Passing a test proves only what it exercises. For performance claims, account for warm-up, variability, and environment differences. Keep artifacts minimal and avoid unnecessary private data.

Return one evidence verdict: verified, not verified, or inconclusive. Include the claim, reproduction method, observed result, evidence location, and limitations. Failure to measure is inconclusive; an observed contradiction is not verified. Do not demand a before-and-after comparison for a claim that needs no baseline.
