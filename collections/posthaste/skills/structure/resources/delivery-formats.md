# Delivery formats

Apply the chosen content type inside the requested container. These are editorial layouts, not statements of current platform limits. Do not copy numeric limits or recommended word counts from the research into a claim of current compliance. If exact fit is requested, verify the current limit and counting rules for the target platform and account mode, then count the complete draft, including links and numbering where applicable.

## Blog or article

Specific title; opening answer, promise, or narrative tension; body organised by the content type; useful ending. Use descriptive headings where they help navigation. Medium and LinkedIn articles can use this layout with the same underlying structure. A longform essay may reveal its thesis progressively. Add a summary only when it serves the reader, and a call to action only when intended.

## Social post

Opening insight; minimal context; one central point; concrete evidence or example; takeaway. An optional genuine question or action can close it when the draft's purpose calls for one. Compress repetition before removing qualification. Do not turn every sentence into an isolated paragraph or replace substance with a suspense hook.

## Thread

Opening point and scope; connected units with one useful idea per unit; evidence near the claim it supports; synthesis; sources where needed. Split at semantic boundaries, not arbitrary character offsets. Number only when useful. The thread retains the selected content type: a tutorial remains a guided exercise, and a case study still needs results. If one post suffices, recommend that instead of stretching it.

## Email newsletter

Subject; optional preheader; strongest value first; main piece in the selected structure; optional secondary modules; relevant next step; sign-off. Preserve a supplied footer. Do not invent subscription information, legal wording, recurring sections, or a next-issue promise.

## LinkedIn newsletter edition

Issue title; optional concise summary; main article; established recurring sections if supplied; takeaway or intended action. Treat this as an edition in an article series, not an email requiring an invented preheader and footer.

## Neutral draft

When no channel change is requested, retain the source's delivery format. If none is identifiable, use readable Markdown: a title when useful, natural paragraphs, and headings or lists suited to the content type. State that the delivery format is a neutral draft. Do not assume a network, audience, or character budget from the collection name.
